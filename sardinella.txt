Dataset:  sardinella.dat

Source: D.E. Wohlshag (1957). "Differences in Metabolic Rates of Migratory
and Resident Freshwater Forms of an Arctic Whitefish," Ecology,
Vol. 38, pp. 502-510

Description: Measurements made on specimens of Coregonus Sardinella
in Elson Lagoon (marine), and Ikroavik Lake (freshwater). Variables include:
log(O2 consumption)
log(weight(grams))
log(mg O2 consumed/hr/kg)
Rotations per minute
Temp (Celsius)
ANCOVA for log(O2) by environment, with log(weight) as covariate
ANCOVA for log(mg O2/hr/day) by environment with RPM as covariate
Other Mult Regs fit also.
