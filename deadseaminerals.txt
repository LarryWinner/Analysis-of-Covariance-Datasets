Dataset:  deadseaminerals.csv

Source: Z. Ma'Or, S. Yehuda, and W. Voss (1997). "Skin Smoothing Effects of
Dead sea Minerals: Comparative Profilometric Evaluation of Skin Surface,"
International Journal of Cosmetic Science, Vol. 19, pp.105-110.

Description: Analysis of Covariance comparing 3 treatments (pre/post)
with response being skin roughness (lower scores are better).
Treatments 1=Gel Formulation, 2=Gel+1% Dead Sea Concentrate, 3=Placebo
Data generated to match means SDs, mins, maxs, correlations.

Variable Names:
trt_Grp
pre_x    (baseline score)
post_y   (4 weeks post trt score)
gel      (Dummy variable for gel trt)
gelDS    (Dummy variable for gel + Dead Sea Concentrate trt)
