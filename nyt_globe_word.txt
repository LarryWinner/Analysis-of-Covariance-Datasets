Dataset:  nyt_globe_word.dat

Source: J. Dilevko and K. Grewal (1998). "Neutrality and Media Literacy
  at the Reference desk: A Case Study," The Journal of Academic Librarianship,
  Vol. 24, Issue 1, pp. 21-32.

Description: Word Counts for articles in NY Times and re-prints in Globe 
for articles in 5 Categories (1=Atts,Lifestyle,Personality, 2=Business,
3=Science/Technology,4=International Politics,5=Environment,Race,Gender)
Authors fit ANCOVA relating word count difference (NYT-Globe) to category
and NYT word count. One article in category 5 was not included. Square
root transformation was applied to word count difference.

Variables/Columns
Category    8
Category 1 Dummy   16
Category 2 Dummy   24
Category 3 Dummy   32
Category 4 Dummy   40
Category 5 Dummy   48
NYT Word Count     52-56
Word Count Difference (NYT - Globe)   60-64
