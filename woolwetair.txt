Dataset:  woolwetair.dat

Source: J. Lindberg (1953). "Relationship Between Various Surface Properties
of Wool Fibers: Part I: Methods for Estimating Wool Fiber Modification,"
Textitle Research Journal, Vol.23 #2, pp. 67-77

Description: Square Roots of Hookean Slope in water (Y) and Hookean
Slope in 65% Relative Humidity for 10 replicates of wool fiber under
six treatments(untreated, Ether-extracted, Ether and alcohol-extracted,
alcoholic potash @ 15 sce, AP@4min, AP@15min). Regression fit for
each treatment (ANCOVA) is: Y=b0+B1*X
