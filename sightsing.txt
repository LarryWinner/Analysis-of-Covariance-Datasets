Dataset:  sightsing.dat

Source: D. Hutton (1953). "A Comparative Study of Two Methods of Teaching
Sight Singing in the Fourth Grade," Journal of Research in Music Education,
Vol.1 #2, pp.119-126.

Description: Pre- and Post-Training scores for 20 kids assigned
to a control group, and 20 in an experimental condition.

Variables/Columns
Treatment Group  8  /* 1=Control, 2=Experimental  */
Initial Test Score  13-16
Final Test Score   21-24
