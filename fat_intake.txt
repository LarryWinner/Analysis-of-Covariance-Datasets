Dataset:  fat_intake.csv

Source1: J. Brug, K. Glanz, P. Van Assema, G. Kok, G.J.P. van Breukelen (1998).
"The Impact of Computer-Tailored Feedback and Iterative Feedback on Fat, Fruit,
and Vegetable Intake," Health Education & Behavior, Vol. 28, #4, pp. 517-531.

Source2: B. Winkens, G.J.P. van Breukelen, H.J.A. Schouten, M.P.F. Berger (2007).
"Randomized Clinical Trials with a Pre- and Post-Treatment Measurement:
Repeated Measures Versus ANCOVA Models." Contemporary Clinical Trials. Vol. 28, pp. 713-719.

Description:
Comparison of 3 informational treatments with respect to fat, fruit, and vegetable intake.
Fat intake measurements made pre- and (second) post-treatment. 
Data generated to match means, sds, r.
Treatments: 1=General Information (GI), 2=Tailored Feedback (TF), 3=Iterative Feedback (IF))

Variable Names: 
infoGrp  (1,2,3)
idGrp    (subject ID within group)
preTrt   (X)
postTrt  (Y)
